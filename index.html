Before you flash

This is an unofficial, community-made modification for education and preservation. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the module's manufacturer. Flashing modified firmware is at your own risk and may affect your warranty.

Keep your original Sealegs.1.3.5.0.bin. To go back to stock, flash it again with the official update procedure.

How it works

The page holds only the added code and a handful of changed constants: no part of the official firmware is included. After your file passes the version check, the patches are written into a copy in memory. The result is then checked against the expected SHA-256 for your exact mod combination.

The patches were verified in an ARM Cortex-M7 emulator running the firmware's own DSP code. Reverse, character and the stability fixes were also tested on hardware. Some newer combinations (reverse without character, character without reverse) have only been verified in emulation.
